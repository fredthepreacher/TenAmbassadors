# National Benchmark Notes

The first-layer strategy is informed by three national education/leadership nonprofit patterns:

## UNCF
Useful pattern: immediate scholarship and donation pathways, strong impact metrics, and a clear "apply / give" conversion structure.
Source: https://www.uncf.org/

## Thurgood Marshall College Fund (TMCF)
Useful pattern: distinct user pathways for students, donors, and partners; scholarship opportunities are browsable and action-oriented; programs extend beyond scholarship into leadership and career development.
Sources: https://tmcf.org/ and https://tmcf.org/scholarships/

## Jackie Robinson Foundation (JRF)
Useful pattern: scholarship is connected directly to mentoring, leadership development, career guidance, community service, and international exposure. This is especially relevant to the Ten Ambassadors SMS model.
Sources: https://jackierobinson.org/scholarship/ and https://jackierobinson.org/apply/

## Ten Ambassadors opportunity
Do not copy any of these sites visually. Combine their strongest structural principles with Ten Ambassadors' existing language and event photography:

1. **Clear action pathways** — Apply / Mentor / Serve / Partner / Give.
2. **Impact proof** — real statistics, outcomes, stories, and partner logos.
3. **SMS as product architecture** — Scholarship, Mentorship, and Service become three program pathways, not just three words in a mission statement.
4. **Editorial photography** — real community images carry the emotional weight of the brand.
5. **Global-minded presentation** — premium typography, generous spacing, motion used carefully, and messaging that can grow beyond one city.
