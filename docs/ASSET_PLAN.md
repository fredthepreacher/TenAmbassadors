## Final enhancement rule
Keep documentary integrity. Enhance presentation, not people.

Allowed final work:
- denoise
- exposure and white-balance correction
- mild sharpening
- resolution improvement
- crop and responsive framing
- remove screenshot chrome/white margins only when reconstructing from an approved original image

Do not:
- alter facial structure or skin tone
- change body shape
- add/remove people
- create fake moments that did not occur
- replace event participants with generated people

Ask for original JPG/PNG files for every featured photograph, event video, logo, partner logo, scholarship recipient photo, and Starlight Awards media asset.
